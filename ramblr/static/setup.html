FIRST-RUN SETUP five short steps; everything can be changed later
1 · CHECKS

Is everything running?

2 · MIC

Which microphone?

Ramblr records it with its own stream; your system default mic is never changed.

3 · YOUR VOICE

Name your voice

Say a sentence or two, wait a few seconds, then name the voice Ramblr just heard and confirm it is you. Only your voice can then say "Larry, ...".

4 · QUIET HOURS

When to flag talk as late-night

5 · OPTIONAL

Extras you can add later

• iPhone capture: /iphone
• Encrypted nightly backup: RAMBLR_BACKUP_HOST (default whi)
• Calendar overlay, AcoustID, voice router, webhooks: secrets in ~/.config/ramblr/secrets.env (see README)
