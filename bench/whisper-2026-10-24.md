# Whisper on the NPU: base.en vs small.en vs distil-large-v3 (2026-10-24, read-only benchmark)

117 room-mic clips from the 24 h store (segments 9-133: 44 household talk, 73 TV/news; 776 s), plus the JFK clip. vic clips do not exist: remote mics never write audio. No hand-corrected text exists yet, so WER is measured against whisper-large-v3 (int8, CPU, nice 19; 452 s for the set) and cross-checked against small.en, which was not distilled from large-v3. Text is scored after the live loop guard. Numbers only: no household transcripts in git.

## Medians (robust to the TV clips where the reference itself writes almost nothing)

Caveat: distil was trained to imitate large-v3, so the large-v3 numbers favour it; the independent small.en check agrees
in direction (smaller gap). On 19 mostly-TV clips distil writes 2.5x+ the reference's words (large-v3 often writes
nearly nothing for noisy news), so TV accuracy is unresolved; TV lines are hidden anyway.

## Decision (Fred 8:36 AM: "What ever is the best is the best")

Switched live to **distil-large-v3**: measurably best on household talk, fewest loops (1 raw vs 6), RTF 0.25 per stream
(talk 0.47 on short clips) = ~0.3 NPU duty per stream at real talk occupancy, ~0.55 with room + vic. small.en was a
talk tie at 2.3x the memory. Rollback: delete RAMBLR_WHISPER / RAMBLR_ASR_MAX_TOKENS from ~/.config/ramblr/config.env.
